Google Pixel phones don't show the volume percentage on the volume slider when changing volume. When listening to music, I like to know how loud the volume is, especially while changing songs. I had to switch to Poweramp Equalizer all the time just to see how loud the volume was currently. I didn't want to use custom volume slider apps, so I decided to create a simple app that just shows the volume percentage in the status bar/notification panel and on AOD.
